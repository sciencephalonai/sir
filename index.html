Executive Summary

Deletions can decide 50+ seats. The window to act is now.

The Mechanism

How Silent Deletion Works

BLOs visit 46,165 booths. Absent households — migrants, fishermen, seasonal workers — get marked "not traceable." Form 7 bulk objections are filed against valid voters. Names vanish in the draft roll before anyone notices.

The Statutory Window

Claims & Objections: The Only Remedy

After the draft roll publishes, a statutory window allows Form 6 filings and Form 7 contests. Miss that window and the deletion is permanent. The platform exists to catch every case in time.

These deletions happened before SIR 2026. SIR is a larger, nationwide exercise with broader scope and ECI institutional backing. The 2026 exposure is materially higher.

SciEncephalonAIVoter Protection Mission · May 2026 05 · The Proposal06 / 14
Parallel Special Intensive Revision

A shadow enumeration that catches every wrongful deletion before it's permanent.

01 · Shadow Enumeration

Build the Counter-Database First

46,165 Voter Protection Volunteers capture every valid voter through the party's karyakarta network. Stored in a single authoritative PostgreSQL database on AWS Mumbai — immutable and auditable.

02 · Real-Time Reconciliation

Match Against the ECI Draft Roll

The moment ECI publishes the draft roll, the engine cross-references every name. Every missing voter triggers an automatic Form 6 filing within the statutory window — no manual intervention.

03 · Form 7 Shield

Counter Bulk Objections in 24 Hours

ML model trained on 2024 AP deletion patterns detects bulk false Form 7 objections within 24 hours. Counter-filings go automatically. AP High Court writs ready for permanent deletions — 72-hour filing.

What This Is
A voter protection platform. Stores only what is on the public electoral roll. No persuasion, no targeting, no political data. No caste. No religion. No political preference.
What This Is Not
Not a vendor engagement. Not a campaign tool. Sunsets 30 days after the final roll publishes. Full open-source codebase handover on day 91. No lock-in.
Core thesis:the party needs a database and alarm system ready before the draft roll publishes. That is what we are building. The window is the next 90 days.

Platform Architecture — Six Pillars

Every deletion pathway covered.

01

02

46,165 VPVs. Mobile app for geo-tagged BLO visit logging, household skip flagging, irregularity reporting. Offline-first, local SQLite cache.

03
Dashboard

Deletion Heatmap

Mandal-level dashboard. Booths with >3% deletion auto-flagged red. Daily 6 AM PDF brief per constituency in-charge. Cloudflare WAF protection.

04
ML Defense

Form 7 Abuse Shield

XGBoost trained on 2024 AP patterns. Detects bulk false objections within 24 hours. Sentence-BERT for fuzzy Telugu name matching across transliterations.

05
Outreach

Migrant Worker Camps

Pre-SIR enrollment at Bengaluru & Hyderabad construction sites, Godavari & Nellore fishing harbours, Mumbai Telugu communities — before BLOs find empty homes.

06
Legal

Legal Fast-Track

Lawyers on standby. Template writs ready. AP High Court within 72 hours of any wrongful final deletion. Party legal cell coordinates statutory filings.

Coverage logic:Pillar 5 handles absent households · Pillar 2 flags BLO irregularities · Pillars 1+3 surface deletions · Pillar 4 counters Form 7 attacks · Pillar 6 litigates what slips through.
