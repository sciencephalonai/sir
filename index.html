The Mechanism

How Silent Deletion Works

BLOs visit 46,165 booths. Absent households — migrants, fishermen, seasonal workers — get marked as "not traceable." Form 7 bulk objections are filed against valid voters. Names disappear in the draft roll before anyone notices.

The Window

Claims & Objections: The Only Legal Remedy

After the draft roll publishes, there is a statutory window to file Form 6 (enroll) and contest Form 7 objections. Miss that window and the deletion is permanent. The platform exists to catch every case in time.

The Proposal — Parallel Special Intensive Revision

A shadow enumeration that catches every wrongful deletion before it becomes permanent.

ECI runs the official process. We do not interfere. We run a parallel system — owned by YSRCP, engineered by SciEncephalon, staffed by KLM — that reconciles against the draft roll the moment it publishes.

01 · Shadow Enumeration

Build the Counter-Database First

46,165 Voter Protection Volunteers capture every valid voter at the booth level through the party's existing karyakarta network. Stored in a single authoritative PostgreSQL database on AWS Mumbai — immutable, auditable, zero data leakage.

02 · Real-Time Reconciliation

Match Against the ECI Draft Roll

The moment ECI publishes the draft roll, the reconciliation engine cross-references every name in the shadow database. Every missing voter triggers an automatic Form 6 filing pipeline within the statutory claims window — no manual intervention needed.

03 · Form 7 Shield

Detect and Counter Bulk Objections

An ML model trained on documented 2024 AP deletion patterns detects bulk false Form 7 objections within 24 hours. Counter-filings go out automatically. Template writs to AP High Court are ready for permanent deletions — 72-hour filing.

What This Is
A voter protection platform. Built to detect and contest wrongful deletion within the statutory window. Stores only what is already on the public electoral roll. No persuasion, no targeting, no political data.
What This Is Not
Not a vendor engagement. Not a campaign tool. Does not store caste, religion, or political preference. Does not operate outside Indian sovereign territory. Not a surveillance system — it sunsets 30 days after the final roll publishes.
Core thesis:the party does not need to fight SIR — it needs a database and an alarm system ready before the draft roll publishes. We are building that system. The window to act is the next 90 days.

Platform Architecture — Six Pillars

Six interlocking systems. Every deletion pathway covered.

Each pillar addresses a specific failure mode in the BLO-visit process. Together they close every gap between the official enumeration and the final roll.

01
WhatsApp

Telugu Alert Bot

Voter types their EPIC number. Bot returns roll status instantly. Auto-alerts when draft roll publishes and the voter's name is missing. Meta WhatsApp Business API, full Telugu language support.

02
Ground Network

BLO Watch Network

One Voter Protection Volunteer per booth. 46,165 VPVs total. Mobile app for geo-tagged BLO visit logging, household skip flagging, and irregularity reporting. Offline-first with local SQLite cache.

03
Dashboard

Deletion Heatmap

Public-facing mandal-level dashboard. Booths with >3% deletion auto-flagged red. Daily PDF brief at 6 AM to constituency in-charges. React + Recharts, Cloudflare WAF for DDoS protection.

04
ML Defense

Form 7 Abuse Shield

XGBoost model trained on documented 2024 AP deletion patterns. Detects bulk false objections within 24 hours. Triggers counter-filing workflow automatically. Sentence-BERT for fuzzy Telugu name matching.

05
Outreach

Migrant Worker Camps

Pre-SIR enrollment camps at Bengaluru, Hyderabad construction sites, Godavari and Nellore fishing harbours, and Mumbai Telugu communities — capturing voters before BLOs find empty homes.

06
Legal

Legal Fast-Track

Lawyers on standby. Template writs ready. AP High Court filings within 72 hours of any wrongful final deletion. Party legal cell coordinates filings within statutory windows.

Coverage logic:missing-household risk is addressed by Pillar 5. BLO visit irregularities by Pillar 2. Draft-roll deletions by Pillars 1 and 3. Bulk Form 7 attacks by Pillar 4. Permanent deletions that slip through by Pillar 6.
